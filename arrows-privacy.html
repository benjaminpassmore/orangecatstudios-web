Arrows Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Arrows is a puzzle game made by Orange Cat Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Arrows on iPhone, iPad or Android.

The short version

Arrows has no account and no sign-in. It shows optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, when you ask for a hint or a continue. It offers a one-time Lifetime purchase that removes them. The ad and purchase providers receive some device information, described below. Your game progress stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

To keep your place and your choices, Arrows saves on your device:

  • your progress: the furthest level reached, the level to continue, and which levels you cleared flawlessly;
  • your settings: sound, vibration and theme;
  • your stats: wins, flawless wins and streaks, arrows launched, mistakes, hints and continues used;
  • whether you've seen the tutorial.

This information never leaves your device. We can't see it, and it isn't linked to you. It's removed when you delete the app. You can clear your stats at any time in Settings.

Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google backups) may include the app's saved data, under Apple's or Google's terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a video for a hint or a continue, Arrows asks Google AdMob for an ad. To do that, Google's software in the app collects and processes:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID: the IDFA on iPhone and iPad (only if you allow tracking), Android's advertising ID, and an app-set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate approximate location;
  • device and app information: model, operating system version, language, app version;
  • ad interactions: views, taps and completed videos;
  • diagnostics: performance and crash data from the ads software.

Google uses this to serve and measure ads and to protect against fraud, as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, Google's consent message asks for your choices before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choices later in the app's Settings.
  • iPhone and iPad. Arrows asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA isn't shared and ads are not personalised.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • Lifetime. Players who buy Lifetime are never shown ads, and Arrows doesn't start the ads software for them.

Purchases (App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat)

Lifetime is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles payment; we never see your payment details. To check and restore your purchase, Arrows uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID;
  • your purchase receipts and history for Arrows;
  • device and app information: model, operating system version, app version, country or region.

This is used only to confirm and restore purchases. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell your information.

Links

Settings has links to our website (Report a Bug) and to this policy. They open in your browser, and the website's own privacy terms apply there. Arrows itself sends nothing when you tap them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. We don't hold information that identifies you. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use their tools, or contact us and we'll help.

Children

Arrows isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of Arrows changes what it collects, we'll update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: orangecatstudios@proton.me, or playorangecat.com/report-bug.html.
